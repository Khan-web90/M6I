Our Story

A quiet ritual, plated with care.

A neighbourhood dining room devoted to two closely-loved traditions — the deep, aromatic cooking of the subcontinent and the bright, wok-fired language of Chinese kitchens.

M61 is a neighbourhood dining room devoted to two closely-loved traditions: the deep, aromatic cooking of the subcontinent and the bright, wok-fired language of Chinese kitchens.

Our menu is built around slow-simmered karahis, handi, hand-pulled noodles, tandoor-fired breads, and a small library of house juices and shakes. The room is dressed in warm brass, deep crimson, and crisp white linen — designed to make an ordinary evening feel like an occasion.

Whether you arrive for a quick lunch or settle in for a long dinner, we want your visit to feel unhurried, generous, and unmistakably M61.
